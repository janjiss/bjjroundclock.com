Questions

How long is a BJJ match?

Under IBJJF rules, adult matches last 5 minutes for white belts, 6 for blue, 7 for purple, 8 for brown and 10 for black belts.

Is the timer free?

The web timer is free. The app is free to try for 10 days; after that, a one-time purchase unlocks it for good. There is no subscription.

Does it keep running when my phone is locked?

The app does: the countdown stays on the Lock Screen and the bells keep ringing. The web timer needs the tab open and the screen on.

Can I use it for drilling and positional sparring?

Yes. Use the Drilling or Positional presets, and turn on the halfway switch to get a cue to change sides or roles mid-round.

Can I share my timer with my gym?

Yes. Press Share and send the link. It opens the timer with the same rounds, round time and rest.
