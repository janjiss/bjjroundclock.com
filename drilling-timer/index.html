How to use it

Pick one technique per round. Partner A drills for the first minute; at the switch cue, partner B takes over. The 15-second rest is for the coach to call the next technique or for you to change partners.

Why drill against the clock

Counting reps pulls attention away from the movement and lets fast pairs finish early. With a clock, everyone works for the same time, and the number of clean reps you fit into a minute becomes a simple way to track progress.

Adjust it

  • One partner per round: turn the halfway switch off and alternate rounds.
  • Both sides of the body: keep the switch on and change side, not partner, at the cue.
  • Flow drilling: stretch the round to 3 or 4 minutes and take the rest down to 10 seconds.

Questions

What does the halfway switch do?

It plays its own sound and shows SWITCH on the screen in the middle of every round: at 1:00 in a 2-minute round.

How many rounds should a drilling session be?

Ten 2-minute rounds take 22:25: enough for ten techniques, or five techniques done twice.

Can I drill alone with it?

Yes. Use the rounds for solo movements such as hip escapes, technical stand-ups and sprawls, and the switch cue to change side.
