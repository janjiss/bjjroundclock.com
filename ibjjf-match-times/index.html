From 1 January 2027, IBJJF adds a Master 8 division for ages 66 and over, with 4-minute matches.

Rest between matches

The rule book also sets a minimum rest between your matches: one full match length for your division up to the semifinals, and double that before a final. An adult blue belt gets at least 6 minutes, and 12 before the final.

Train at your match length

Rolling at the exact length of your division is the simplest way to learn its pace. Set one round at your belt’s time for a match simulation, or stack several with short rests for competition prep.

The clapper marks the last 30 seconds, so you practise closing out a match without looking at the clock.

Questions

How long is a white belt match in IBJJF?

5 minutes for adults and in every master division. Juvenile white belts, aged 16 and 17, also get 5 minutes.

How long is a black belt match?

10 minutes for adults, 6 minutes in Master 1 and 5 minutes from Master 2 up.

How long are kids’ matches?

2 minutes for ages 4 to 6, 3 minutes for ages 7 to 9 and 4 minutes for ages 10 to 15.

What happens if the score is level when time runs out?

There is no overtime in IBJJF. If points are level, advantages decide, then penalties, then the referees.
