ADCC match times

ADCC uses the same match length for everyone at an event, and plays overtime only when a match ends in a draw.

StageMatchNo pointsOvertime
World Championship, qualifying rounds10 minfirst 5 min5 min
World Championship, finals20 minfirst 10 min*10 min
Trials and Nationals, qualifying rounds6 minfirst 3 min3 min
Trials and Nationals, finals8 minfirst 4 min*4 min

* In finals, negative points count from the start of the match.

Local ADCC Opens can set their own times by division, so check the event page.

Source: ADCC rules and regulations

Why the first half has no points

In qualifying rounds nothing scores in the first half of the match, which rewards going for submissions early. In the second half takedowns, passes and dominant positions score, and negative points apply.

Train for it

Turn on the halfway switch: its cue lands exactly where points start, at 5:00 of a 10-minute round. Roll the first half for submissions and the second half for points, the way the match is scored.

Adjust it

  • Trials pace: set the round to 6:00 and the cue lands at 3:00.
  • Finals: one 20-minute round, with the cue at 10:00.

Questions

How long is an ADCC match?

10 minutes in World Championship qualifying rounds and 20 minutes in the finals. Trials and Nationals use 6 minutes, with 8-minute finals.

Is there overtime in ADCC?

Yes, when a match is a draw: 5 minutes at Worlds, or 10 in the finals, and 3 minutes at Trials, or 4 in the finals. If it is still level, the referees decide.

How is ADCC timing different from IBJJF?

IBJJF match time depends on belt and age, from 5 to 10 minutes for adults, with no overtime. ADCC uses one match length per event and adds overtime for draws.
